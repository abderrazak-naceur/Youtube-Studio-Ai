# Research UI

## Research workspace

- research objective
- source list
- evidence cards
- claims
- confidence
- citations/provenance
- contradictions
- unresolved questions

## Fact-check interaction

Each claim can be:

- Verified
- Partially verified
- Contradicted
- Needs review
- Unverified

Users can open the source evidence directly and approve/reject AI conclusions.

## Brief output

The final research view produces a structured brief containing audience, thesis, evidence, counterarguments, sources, risks and recommended narrative angles.
