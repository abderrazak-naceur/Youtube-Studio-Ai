# v0.36 Workspace Unit Economics

## Purpose

Workspace-level AI CFO view (REVENUE-ENGINE unit economics): total revenue, production cost, contribution and margin, with a per-video breakdown ranked by contribution.

## Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/economics` (workspace-scoped):

- `totalRevenueUsd`, `totalProductionCostUsd`, `totalContributionUsd`, `contributionMargin`
- `videos[]` — per-video revenue, cost and contribution, highest contribution first

Reads `video_projects`, `production_costs` and `revenue_events`; no new tables.

## Verification

Backend coverage: totals and per-video breakdown aggregated and ranked by contribution; zeros for an empty workspace; non-member forbidden.
