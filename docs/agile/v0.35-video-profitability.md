# v0.35 Video Profitability

## Purpose

Per-video unit economics (REVENUE-ENGINE, AI CFO): connect recorded costs and revenue into contribution and margin.

## Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/video-projects/{videoProjectId}/profitability` (workspace-scoped):

- `revenueUsd` — sum of revenue events attributed to the video
- `productionCostUsd` — sum of recorded production costs for the video
- `contributionUsd` — revenue − cost
- `contributionMargin` — contribution ÷ revenue (0 when there is no revenue)

Reads existing `production_costs` and `revenue_events`; no new tables.

## Verification

Backend coverage: contribution and margin computed from costs and revenue; negative contribution with zero margin when there is no revenue; 404 for missing project and non-member.
