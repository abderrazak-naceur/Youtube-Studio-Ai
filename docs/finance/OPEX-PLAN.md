# OPEX Plan

## Main operating expenses

| Category | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | 2030 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| People | Low | Medium | High | Very High | Very High |
| Cloud | Low | Medium | High | Very High | Very High |
| AI | Medium | High | Very High | Very High | Very High |
| Marketing | Low | Medium | High | High | Very High |
| Legal/Finance | Low | Medium | Medium | High | High |
| Media | Medium | Medium | High | Very High | Very High |

Actual amounts must be generated from monthly usage and provider price sheets rather than static assumptions.

## Control system

- monthly budget per department
- purchase approval threshold
- cloud spend alerts
- AI provider spend limits
- cost anomaly alerts
- quarterly vendor review
