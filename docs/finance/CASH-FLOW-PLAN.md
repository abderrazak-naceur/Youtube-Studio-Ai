# Cash Flow Plan

## Monthly cash model

`OpeningCash + CashIn - CashOut = ClosingCash`

## Cash-in

- SaaS subscriptions
- annual contracts
- services
- sponsorships
- affiliate
- YouTube revenue
- financing

## Cash-out

- payroll
- contractors
- cloud
- AI providers
- software
- marketing
- legal/accounting
- taxes
- media production

## Controls

Maintain a 13-week cash forecast and monthly 24-month scenario model.

Track base, downside and upside cases. Trigger spending freezes when runway falls below the approved threshold.
