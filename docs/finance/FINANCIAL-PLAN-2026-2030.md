# Financial Plan 2026–2030

> Internal planning model. Values are scenarios, not forecasts.

## 2026 — validation

Objective: prove production economics.

Target monthly operating envelope: €2k–€15k depending on AI generation volume and contractor usage.

Focus: product, cloud, AI, legal/accounting, prototype media.

## 2027 — first SaaS revenue

Target operating envelope: €15k–€50k/month.

Focus: team, cloud reliability, AI usage, acquisition and customer support.

## 2028 — scale

Target envelope: €50k–€150k/month.

Focus: revenue intelligence, sales, media portfolio and automation.

## 2029 — automation

Target envelope: €100k–€300k+/month.

Focus: international SaaS, enterprise and owned channels.

## 2030 — media company OS

Target envelope: €200k–€600k+/month depending on revenue scale.

## Budget allocation rule

At each stage maintain separate budgets for:

- payroll
- infrastructure
- AI
- media production
- marketing
- sales
- legal/finance
- contingency

Maintain a minimum cash runway target of 12 months once external capital or material recurring revenue exists.
