# Hiring Plan 2026–2030

> Planning ranges, not commitments. Actual hiring follows revenue, runway and bottlenecks.

## 2026 — 1–4 people + contractors

Founder + 1–2 engineering profiles. Fractional design, accounting, legal and media specialists.

Indicative fully loaded annual personnel budget: €50k–€180k depending on founder compensation and contractor mix.

## 2027 — 4–8 people

Engineering, AI, product/design, growth and customer success.

Indicative budget: €250k–€550k/year.

## 2028 — 8–15 people

Add data, DevOps, sales, finance/operations and media production.

Indicative budget: €600k–€1.2m/year.

## 2029 — 15–30 people

Dedicated business units and management.

Indicative budget: €1.2m–€2.8m/year.

## 2030 — 25–60+ people

International SaaS, enterprise, owned media and platform operations.

Indicative budget: €2.5m–€6m+/year.

## Hiring gates

Hire only when one of these is true:

1. Revenue supports the role.
2. The role removes a proven operational bottleneck.
3. The role materially improves product quality or reliability.
4. Outsourcing is more expensive or strategically risky.

Personnel figures exclude taxes/benefits that vary by country and employment structure and must be modeled by jurisdiction.
