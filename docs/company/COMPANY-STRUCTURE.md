# YouTube Studio AI — Company Structure

## Purpose

Define the company required to build, operate and scale YouTube Studio AI as a software company plus an owned AI media business.

## Company model

The company has five operating pillars:

1. Product & Engineering
2. AI & Data
3. Media Operations
4. Growth & Sales
5. Finance, Legal & Operations

## 2026 founder-led structure

The founder acts as CEO/Product Owner/initial business operator. Use contractors selectively for design, legal, accounting and specialist media work.

### Core roles

- Founder / CEO
- Full-stack engineer
- AI/ML engineer
- Product/UI designer (fractional)
- Video/media specialist (fractional)
- Accountant/legal advisor (external)

## 2027 structure

Add:

- CTO/lead engineer
- backend engineer
- frontend engineer
- AI engineer
- product designer
- growth/marketing lead
- customer success

## 2028 structure

Add dedicated:

- data/analytics
- DevOps/cloud
- sales
- finance
- media producer/editor
- partnerships

## 2029–2030

Separate Software, SaaS, Owned Media and Corporate functions into business units.

## Decision principles

- Keep fixed costs low until product-market fit.
- Outsource specialist work before hiring full time.
- Hire for bottlenecks, not titles.
- Every team must have measurable business KPIs.
- AI automation should reduce repetitive operational work before it replaces strategic human judgment.
