# Organization Plan

## Objective

Scale from a founder-led startup to an international software and media company without creating unnecessary management overhead.

## Management cadence

### Weekly
- product delivery review
- production/cost review
- sales pipeline
- incidents
- cash position

### Monthly
- P&L
- cloud and AI spend
- customer acquisition
- retention
- content portfolio
- roadmap decisions

### Quarterly
- strategy review
- budget reallocation
- hiring plan
- product portfolio
- owned-media portfolio

## Operating principles

- One accountable owner per KPI.
- Written decisions for architectural and financial changes.
- Production budgets approved by expected contribution margin.
- No new permanent cost without a business justification.
- Security, legal and publishing gates cannot be bypassed by growth targets.

## Company scorecard

- MRR / ARR
- gross margin
- net burn
- runway
- CAC
- LTV
- retention
- videos/month
- cost/video
- revenue/video
- engineering velocity
- uptime
