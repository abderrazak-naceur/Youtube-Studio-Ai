# Analytics Intelligence

## Objective

Convert channel and video results into decisions that improve future content and business performance.

## Metric layers

### Content metrics
- impressions
- click-through rate
- views
- average view duration
- retention
- watch time
- engagement

### Audience metrics
- returning viewers
- new viewers
- audience segments
- geography/language where available
- subscriber behavior

### Business metrics
- revenue
- RPM/CPM where available
- affiliate conversions
- sponsorship value
- product conversions
- cost per video
- contribution margin

## Diagnostic framework

When performance changes, distinguish:

`Distribution → Packaging → Hook → Content → Audience fit → Monetization`

Do not assume low views mean low content quality; the system should test competing explanations.

## Experimentation

Experiments can target:
- title
- thumbnail
- opening hook
- format
- length
- publishing time
- CTA
- topic cluster

Each experiment must define:
- hypothesis
- variable
- baseline
- success metric
- observation window
- decision rule

## Content Genome feedback

Analytics should update Genome attributes and estimate which combinations correlate with outcomes.

The system must avoid claiming causality from simple correlations. Where experiments are unavailable, recommendations should be expressed as hypotheses.

## Opportunity feedback

Performance should influence future Opportunity Scores:

`topic × audience × format × packaging × monetization × cost`

The goal is a continuously improving opportunity-ranking system.

## AI analyst output

The Analytics Agent should produce:
1. What happened?
2. Why might it have happened?
3. What evidence supports each explanation?
4. What should be tested next?
5. Expected business impact.

## Portfolio analytics

For multiple channels, compare capital allocation by expected incremental business value rather than views alone.
