# Revenue Engine

## Objective

Turn audience attention into diversified, measurable business value while reducing dependence on advertising alone.

## Revenue streams

1. YouTube advertising
2. Affiliate commissions
3. Sponsorships
4. Digital products
5. Newsletter monetization
6. Memberships
7. Shopping/commerce
8. Lead generation
9. Owned SaaS/products
10. Services

## Revenue attribution

Every monetization event should be attributable, when technically possible, to:

`channel → video → content cluster → CTA → conversion → revenue`

Attribution may be probabilistic and should expose uncertainty.

## AI CFO

The AI CFO should answer:
- Which videos are profitable?
- Which topics create the highest contribution margin?
- Which channels deserve more resources?
- What is the cost per finished video?
- What is the expected value of producing the next video?
- Which monetization stream should be tested next?

## Unit economics

Track at minimum:

`Revenue per video - variable production cost - distribution cost - attributable operating cost`

Useful secondary metrics:
- cost per minute produced
- cost per 1,000 views
- revenue per 1,000 views
- contribution margin per video
- payback period
- customer acquisition cost for SaaS
- lifetime value

## Sponsorship engine

Future functionality may score sponsor fit using:
- audience fit
- brand fit
- topic fit
- historical conversion
- expected deal value
- production requirements

No automatic commercial commitment should be made without user authorization.

## Affiliate engine

Support product/entity mapping, disclosure requirements, click/conversion tracking and profitability analysis.

## Digital products

The system should identify repeated audience problems that can justify:
- guides
- templates
- courses
- tools
- newsletters
- SaaS products

The objective is to build owned assets instead of optimizing only for platform revenue.

## Financial guardrails

Any paid operation must respect explicit budgets and require approval when thresholds are exceeded.
