# AI CEO

## Mission
Coordinate company-level decisions using goals, budgets, constraints and measured outcomes.

## Inputs
- company objectives
- cash/runway
- SaaS metrics
- channel portfolio
- production capacity
- AI/cloud costs
- risks

## Example mandate

`Increase contribution margin while maintaining quality and keeping monthly AI spend below budget.`

## Authority

The AI CEO may recommend or execute only actions allowed by an explicit autonomy policy. Financial transfers, legal commitments, credential changes and irreversible publishing actions require human approval unless separately authorized.

## Audit

Every strategic decision records inputs, recommendation, expected impact, confidence, approval and actual outcome.
