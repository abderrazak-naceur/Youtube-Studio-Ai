# Pricing Strategy

## Principle

Price against business value and usage economics, not arbitrary feature counts.

## Initial planning tiers

| Plan | Target | Indicative price |
|---|---|---:|
| Free | Evaluation | €0 |
| Creator | Individual | €19–€39/month |
| Pro | Serious creator | €79–€149/month |
| Business | Small team | €249–€499/month |
| Agency | Multi-channel | €799–€1,999/month |
| Enterprise | Media company | Custom |

These are hypotheses for testing, not final prices.

## Usage economics

Plans should include a fair base allowance plus metered AI/media usage. Expensive generation must never create unlimited liability for the company.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing can combine platform fee, seats, usage commitment, support/SLA and integrations.

## Pricing experiments

Test willingness to pay, activation, retention, gross margin and expansion—not only conversion rate.
