# Funding Plan

## Stage 0 — bootstrapped

Target: prove product and first content economics with minimal fixed costs.

Use founder capital and revenue where possible.

## Stage 1 — pre-seed

Consider external capital only after evidence of:

- repeatable production
- user demand
- early revenue
- measurable retention
- clear cost model

Potential use: engineering, AI infrastructure, product and GTM.

## Stage 2 — seed

Raise when capital can accelerate a validated engine rather than finance unresolved product assumptions.

Potential use: team expansion, enterprise readiness, international growth and owned media portfolio.

## Capital discipline

Never raise based only on AI excitement. Maintain a 12–18 month runway target after a material financing event and model dilution, liquidation preferences and governance with professional legal/financial advice.
